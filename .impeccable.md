# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
NBA fans, stat-heads, and basketball-history nerds who argue about the GOAT. They arrive curious and opinionated — exploring rankings, looking up favorite players, settling debates. Context is leisure browsing (desktop and phone), not work. They reward depth and authenticity, and they instantly distrust anything that looks like a generic dashboard. The product, REIGN, quantifies player impact across 80 years (1946–2025) using era-specific composite models.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **authoritative, archival, opinionated.** REIGN takes a stance ("here is who truly dominated") and backs it with rigor. The voice is that of a confident sports almanac crossed with a data-essay — serious about the numbers, but with the texture and warmth of print history. Emotional goal: make a stat table feel like flipping through a vintage record/almanac collection — discovery, nostalgia, and authority at once.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Vintage editorial / newsprint-zine**, modeled on The Pudding's "What Makes an Album the Greatest of All Time?" (pudding.cool/2024/03/greatest-music). Core moves:
- Warm off-white **newsprint paper** background with subtle grain/noise — never pure white.
- **High-contrast 1-bit / halftone / duotone "photocopied" treatment** on player photos, unifying 1950s B&W and 2025 color shots into one archival artifact. Photos must be sourced via a name→image mapping (season data has no player ID — see constraints).
- **Editorial Didone typography**: the masthead is a lone, centered **REIGN** wordmark set in Bodoni Moda; display figures (REIGN/OFF/DEF) also Bodoni Moda; all body, table, labels in **Spectral**. (Earlier Big Shoulders + Archivo were dropped per user direction toward a fully editorial, magazine-masthead feel.)
- A **tiled wall of treated player "posters"** used as backdrop texture behind the masthead, with a bottom reflection/fade.
- Restrained motion: staggered entrance reveals + subtle parallax (not full scroll-jacking, in v1).

**Palette:** newsprint cream + near-black ink as the 90%, with **REIGN gold (#b8860b family)** as the single signature accent. Era colors (Pioneer/Legacy/Classic/Modern) remain available as secondary data-coded accents. Light theme — derived from the "browsing an almanac in daylight" context, and from the print-history concept itself.

**Anti-references:** generic analytics dashboards, NBA.com's corporate blue, dark-mode-with-neon-glow, cyan-on-dark, card-grid monotony, gradient text. The current build's bright sky-blue + mint (#5DFDCB) Rankings styling is being replaced.

### Design Principles
1. **Print, not dashboard.** Every surface should read like a designed page from an almanac or zine — type-led, grain, ink — not a SaaS UI.
2. **One treatment to rule them all.** The halftone/duotone image filter is the signature; it must be consistent everywhere photos appear so eras visually unify.
3. **Gold is rare.** Near-black on cream carries 90% of the design; REIGN gold appears only on the things that matter (the #1, the hero mark, key emphasis).
4. **Density with rhythm.** This is a stats product — embrace dense data, but break it with generous editorial spacing, asymmetry, and varied scale so it never feels like a spreadsheet.
5. **Motion serves arrival.** Reveals and parallax reward scrolling; they never block reading or hijack the scroll.

### Technical Constraints
- React 19 + Vite 7. Custom SVG charts + Recharts. Data is parallel-loaded era JSON in `/public/data/`. **Season records are keyed by `name` + `team` + `year` — there is NO player-ID slug.** Real-photo sourcing therefore needs either a built name→B-Ref-slug map or a name→headshot-URL map (check `/data/player_index.json` first).
- Photo sourcing must handle: cross-origin (CORS) limits on canvas pixel reads (favor SVG-filter treatment which doesn't taint cross-origin images, or pre-treat at build time), and many Pioneer/Legacy-era players having **no** headshot — needs a graceful treated-crest fallback (existing `playerArt.js` SVG system).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Maintain WCAG AA contrast (ink-on-cream is strong; verify gold-on-cream for small text).
